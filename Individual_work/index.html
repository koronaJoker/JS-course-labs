Death Note

    I

    • The human whose name is written in this note shall die.
    • This note will not take effect unless the writer has the person's face in mind.
    • If the cause of death is written within 40 seconds, it will happen.
    • If the cause is not specified, the person will die of a heart attack.
    • After writing the cause, details should be written in the next 6 minutes and 40 seconds.

    II

    • This note becomes the property of the human world once it arrives there.
    • The owner can recognize the image and voice of its original owner.
    • The human who uses this note can neither go to Heaven nor Hell.
